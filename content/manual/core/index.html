Core

What every game made with the framework is built from: the app that runs the frame, the scenes the game moves between, and the objects that live in them.

  • The app — the entry point, the frame, quitting, and the state that outlives a scene.
  • Scenes — the screens of the game, and the stack that moves between them.
  • Objects — what lives in a scene: spawning, the fields, the hooks, destroying, handles and callbacks.
  • Collision — colliders, solid objects, layers and masks, the edges of the world, the map's solid cells, raycasts.
  • Behaviors — plain structs that make an object move or act a certain way, the catalogue, and writing your own.
  • Camera — what part of the world is on screen: following, smoothing, limits, zoom, shake.
