Help

The framework tries to fail early and say why: a configuration it cannot build is refused before anything compiles, with the line and the reason, and a mistake it can see in a running game is said once, in the log, in words that name the fix. These pages collect what those messages mean and the questions that come up around them.

  • Troubleshooting — real problems, the messages they print, and what to do about each.
  • Questions — what people ask before, or instead of, reading the rest of the manual.

Two commands answer most questions on your own machine. rmp test checks the .toml, the pinned versions and the licences, draws a frame with the software renderer, and boots the game to see it draw. rmp help lists every command, and rmp help test explains one with examples.
