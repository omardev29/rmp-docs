Reference

Every page in this section is generated from the framework's headers, at the commit the footer names. A signature here is the header's signature, a sentence is the header's comment, and every entry links to the line that declares it.

  • Namespaces — rmp and its modules: rmp::ui, rmp::input, rmp::assets and the rest, each with its functions, its types and its options.
  • Classes — the types with pages of their own: rmp::Scene, rmp::Object, rmp::Camera and the others.
  • Behaviors — the catalogue in rmp::behavior.
  • Macros — RMP_GAME and the rest.

Two conventions hold everywhere. A name starting with _ is a hook: you override it, the framework calls it. A name in detail — rmp::detail, or a member called detail_something — belongs to the framework and its tests, and is not shown.
