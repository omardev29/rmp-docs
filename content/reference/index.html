Reference

Every page in this section is generated from the framework, at the commit the footer names. The names come from its headers: a signature here is the header's signature, a sentence is the header's comment, and every entry links to the line that declares it.

Two pages come from the framework's tools instead, run rather than parsed, so what they say is what the tool does: the configuration keys from tools/configure.py --print-schema, explained by the comments of the framework's own .toml, and the commands from rmp help. The values the .toml turns into macros, on the macros page, come from configure.py the same way. The macros the build passes to the compiler have no header to be read from: their entries are written for this site, and checked against the build files that set them.

  • Namespaces — rmp and its modules: rmp::ui, rmp::input, rmp::assets and the rest, each with its functions, its types and its options.
  • Classes — the types with pages of their own: rmp::Scene, rmp::Object, rmp::Camera and the others.
  • Behaviors — the catalogue in rmp::behavior.
  • Macros — RMP_GAME and the rest: the ones the headers define, the ones the .toml generates and the ones the build passes.
  • Configuration — every key of raylib_multiplatform.toml, with its default.
  • Commands — every rmp command.

Two conventions hold everywhere. A name starting with _ is a hook: you override it, the framework calls it. A name in detail — rmp::detail, or a member called detail_something — belongs to the framework and its tests, and is not shown.
