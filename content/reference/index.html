Reference

Every page in this section is generated from the framework's headers, at the commit the footer names. A signature here is the header's signature, a sentence is the header's comment, and every entry links to the line that declares it.

  • rmp::Scene — a scene, and the stack of scenes.
  • rmp::Camera — what part of the world a scene shows.

Two conventions hold everywhere. A name starting with _ is a hook: you override it, the framework calls it. A name in detail — rmp::detail, or a member called detail_something — belongs to the framework's own tests and is not shown.
