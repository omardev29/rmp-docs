The framework

What the framework is made of, and the reasons behind the shape it has. You do not need any of this to make a game; it is here for when you want to know why.

Three things, in short. The rmp library, compiled once and linked by your game: the headers under include/rmp/, one per module, and their implementation under src/rmp/. One configuration file, raylib_multiplatform.toml, which tools/configure.py turns into what CMake, Gradle and Xcode read, so they cannot disagree with it. And CI that builds the game for {{count:targets}} targets, starts it, and checks that it drew.
